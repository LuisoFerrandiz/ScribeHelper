Protest nr: 02
Class FORMULA KITE Men
Protestor SGP MM1 - Maximilian Maeder
Protestee FRA ND26 - Nell De Jaham
Witnesses
Type protest
Incident SGP Sailing starboard downwind, FRA sailing port upwind towards SGP.
SGP took avoiding action to FRA on port.
Date/Time of hearing 20 Apr 2026 at 15:15
Location Jury Rooms Base Nautique 1st floor
Procedural matters 1. SGP MM1 was represented by Maximilian Maeder.
2. FRA ND26 was represented by Nell De Jaham.
3. Arnaud Mante (rule advisor for SGP MM1) and Colin Oudot (coach of
FRA ND26) attended the hearing as an observers.
4. SGP MM1 presented video evidence.
Facts found 1. Wind 6-7 kts, flat waters.
2. SGP MM1 having rounded mark 1 was sailing downwind on starboard
tack.
3. FRA ND26 sailing upwind on port tack.
4. Immediately before crossing, SGP MM1 lifted her kite more than 10
degrees and her kite passed over the control lines of FRA ND26.
5. No contact, injury or tangle occurred.
6. No kiteboard took a penalty.
Conclusion 1. There was reasonable apprehension there would be a contact between
SGP MM1 and FRA ND26 if SGP MM1 had not taken avoiding action (see
WS Case 50).
2. FRA ND26 on port tack failed to keep clear of SGP MM1 on starboard
tack, and broke RRS 10.
Rules applicables RRS 10, WS Case 50
Jury Decision: FRA is DSQ from race 2
Date of decision: 20 Apr 2026 at 16:18
