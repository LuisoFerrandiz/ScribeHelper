Class Nacra 17
Protestor ESP 489 - Joan Cardona
Protestee NED 22 - Willemijn Offerman
Witnesses
Type protest
Incident We were sailing on starboard tack, on the starboard layline. NED22 came
on port tack and we had to avoid them.
Date/Time of hearing 20 Apr 2026 at 17:20
Location Jury Rooms Base Nautique 1st floor
Procedural matters 1. The panel of the International Jury was composed in accordance with
RRS N1.4(b).
2. ESP 489 was represented by Joan CARDONA.
3. NED 22 was represented by Scipio HOUTMAN.
4. Diego Yubero (ESP support person) attended the hearing as an
observer.
Facts found 1. The conditions at the time of the incident were 6 knots of wind and flat
water.
2. On the second upwind leg, approaching the windward mark on the
starboard layline, ESP 489 was sailing close-hauled on starboard tack.
3. NED 22 was sailing close-hauled on port tack, on a converging course
with ESP 489.
4. NED 22 held her course.
5. ESP 489 bore away sharply and ESP 489’s starboard hull passed over
NED 22’s stern.
6. There was no contact, damage or injury.
7. Neither boat took a penalty.
Conclusion NED 22 on port tack failed to keep clear of ESP 489 on starboard tack, and
broke RRS 10.
Rules applicables RRS 10
Jury Decision: NED 22 is DSQ in race 2.
